Common questions

Is the free plan limited in what it can produce?

No. The signature you build on the free plan is the same markup a paid plan produces — every template, control and export option is available. What you pay for is managing many people's signatures together, not a better signature.

What does "hosted images" actually buy me?

On the free plan, an uploaded logo is embedded directly into the signature. That previews correctly but gets stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so recipients see a broken image — you need to host the file somewhere yourself and paste the URL.

Paid plans host your logo and icon set for you, which also means circular icon badges can be served as images and keep their shape in Outlook.

Can staff still edit their own details?

That is what the section locks control. Typically a team locks typography, brand colour and the legal disclaimer, and leaves contact fields editable so each person can set their own direct line and mobile number.

What happens to our signatures if we stop paying?

Signatures already installed in people's mail clients keep working — they are plain HTML sitting in Outlook or Gmail settings, not something served from here. You would lose central management and hosted image serving, so you would need to rehost your logo before those images expired.
