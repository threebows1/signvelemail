Common questions

What happens when the 30 days are up?

Nothing is deleted and nothing is charged — no card is taken to begin with. Your account stays, your signatures stay, and you pick a plan when you are ready. A logo you have already hosted carries on being served, so mail you sent during the trial does not decay. What needs a plan is uploading a new image or replacing one.

Does a cheaper plan produce a worse signature?

No. Solo, Team and Business all produce identical markup, with all seventeen templates and every design control. The difference is how many signatures the plan covers and whether you get the tools for keeping other people's consistent — shared brand defaults, locked sections, directory sync.

What does "hosted images" actually buy me?

Without it, an uploaded logo is embedded directly into the signature. That previews correctly but gets stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so recipients see a broken image — you would need to host the file somewhere yourself and paste in the URL.

Every plan, and the trial, hosts your logo and icon set for you. It also means circular icon badges can be served as images, which is the only way they keep their shape in Outlook.

Can staff still edit their own details?

That is what the section locks control. Typically a team locks typography, brand colour and the legal disclaimer, and leaves contact fields editable so each person can set their own direct line and mobile number.

What happens to our signatures if we stop paying?

Signatures already installed in people's mail clients keep working — they are plain HTML sitting in Outlook or Gmail settings, not something served from here. Logos already hosted keep being served as well, so nothing you have sent goes broken behind you and there is no image to rehost. What stops is central management, and uploading or replacing a hosted image.
