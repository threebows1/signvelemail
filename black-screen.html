Black Screen

Keep a video playing behind a black screen that blocks every touch. Listen with the screen dark, save battery, and stop stray taps from pausing, skipping or closing what you're watching.

Everything you need, nothing you don't

A black screen that keeps playing

Open YouTube or any video app, tap the floating button, and the screen goes black while the video and sound carry on.

Every touch blocked

Taps, swipes from the edge, the back button and, if you like, the volume buttons do nothing until you tap Unlock.

Your clock, your way

A dim clock in the font, colour and size you choose, with the date and battery level. It moves a little to protect OLED screens.

Sleep timer

Pause the video after 15 to 90 minutes, then let the phone sleep. It also lets the screen sleep if nothing has played for five minutes.

Lock from anywhere

Floating button, Quick Settings tile, notification, app shortcut, or one tap from your home screen.

See what you saved

How often and how long you used it, and a rough estimate of the battery a black screen saved.

Common questions

Does the video really keep playing?

Yes. The black screen sits on top of the video app instead of switching away from it, so YouTube and other players keep going. Keep "Keep screen awake" on: most video apps stop when the phone itself goes to sleep.

Is it safe to give to a child?

It stops most accidental taps, but Android still lets anyone swipe up to go home, so it is a convenience, not a parental-control system. For young children, also use Android's App pinning (Settings › Security › App pinning).

What does Pro cost?

Pro is available monthly, yearly or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in your currency in the app and on Google Play, and you can cancel a subscription any time in Google Play.

What data does it collect?

No account, no ads, no advertising ID. Anonymous usage statistics (which features are used) help us improve the app and can be switched off in the app. See the privacy policy for details.
