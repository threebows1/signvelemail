(03) The difficult part

Most signatures break in Outlook.

Outlook renders mail through Word. It ignores rounded corners, drops inline SVG, and undoes half of what a browser would accept. Gmail strips background images and cuts a long message off entirely. A signature that looks right in the builder and arrives broken is worse than no signature at all.

  • Nested tables, inline styles No flexbox, no grid, no stylesheet — the only markup every client agrees on.
  • Images hosted, not embedded Uploads become real URLs. The data: URIs a browser produces are stripped before the recipient sees them.
  • Checked against the clients Every layout rendered and read in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, Yahoo, Thunderbird and Proton.
  • Falls back rather than breaks No photo, no logo, images switched off — the layout still reads as a finished signature.
