Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026. This policy explains what information Sign Vel collects when you use signvel.com and the Sign Vel editor, and how it is used.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and the name and profile photo you choose to add.
  • Your signatures: the details you type into a signature (such as name, job title, phone numbers, address and links) and the settings you choose.
  • Images you upload: logos, photos and banners, which we host so they show up in the emails you send.
  • Plan details: which plan or trial your account is on, and its dates.
  • Usage information: pages visited and general device and browser information, collected through Google Analytics.

How we use it

To run your account, save and show your signatures, host your images, keep the service secure, answer your messages, and understand how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell your information.

Who helps us run Sign Vel

  • Supabase stores accounts, signatures and uploaded images.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and serves your hosted images.
  • Google Analytics measures visits to the site.
  • Google Fonts provides the fonts the pages use.

They process information only to provide their service to us.

Images in your emails

A hosted image in your signature is fetched by your recipients’ mail apps each time an email is opened. That is what lets an image show, and lets a campaign banner switch off after its end date.

Cookies and local storage

Sign Vel uses your browser’s storage to keep you signed in and to remember editor settings on your device. Google Analytics uses cookies to count visits.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. To have your account and its signatures and images deleted, or to ask what we hold about you, email info@signvel.com.

Children

Sign Vel is a business tool and is not meant for children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: info@signvel.com.
