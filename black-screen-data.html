Black Screen · Your data

Your data and how to delete it

Black Screen has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. This page explains the little data there is and how to remove it. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Data on your phone

Your settings and the totals in the Stats tab are stored only on your phone. To delete them:

  • Just the stats: in the app, open the Stats tab and tap Reset stats.
  • Everything: Android Settings › Apps › Black Screen › Storage › Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Anonymous usage statistics

If you agreed to share usage data when you first opened the app (or later switched on Share usage data), the app sends anonymous statistics about which features are used to Google Firebase, linked to a random ID for that installation (not to your name).

  • Stop it: in the app, open the Stats tab and switch off Share usage data. Nothing more is sent from then on.
  • Delete what was sent: in the app, open the Stats tab, scroll to About and tap Copy usage ID. Email it to mysecondwish11@gmail.com with the subject "Black Screen data deletion". We delete the data linked to that ID from Google Analytics within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Start fresh: uninstalling the app or clearing its storage gives it a new random ID, so later statistics are no longer linked to earlier ones.

Data that isn't deleted on request is removed automatically by Google Analytics at the end of the retention period set for the app.

Purchases

Black Screen Pro purchases are processed and kept by Google Play under your Google account; we don't hold your payment details. To see or manage them, use the Google Play Store (profile picture › Payments & subscriptions). For Google's data, see your Google Account's data & privacy settings.
