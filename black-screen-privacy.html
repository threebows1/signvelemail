Black Screen Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026. This policy covers the Black Screen app for Android (package com.mysecondwish.blackscreen), made by Sign Vel. In short: the app sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Firebase (which you can switch off), and Google Play handles Black Screen Pro purchases. It never collects your name, contacts, screen contents or advertising ID.

What the app collects

Black Screen has no accounts and no advertising, and Sign Vel runs no server for it. To learn which features are used and improve the app, it uses Google Analytics for Firebase, which receives:

  • Feature use: for example that the screen was locked, from where (floating button, tile, shortcut), which mode and options were on, roughly how long it lasted (in ranges such as â€œ10 to 30 minutesâ€), which settings were changed, and whether the Pro page was opened or a purchase started.
  • Standard app information that Firebase collects automatically: an app-instance ID (a random ID for this installation, not linked to your name), app version, device model, Android version, country/region derived from the connection, and when the app was first opened and used.

It does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, screen contents, which video you watch, or your advertising ID; the advertising ID and ad-related permissions are removed from the app and ad personalisation is switched off. You can stop this at any time in the app under Stats > Share usage data. Firebase is provided by Google; see Privacy and Security in Firebase. Google keeps this data for the retention period set in Firebase (no longer than 14 months). To stop it, or to have what was sent deleted, see Your data.

Black Screen Pro purchases

Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details; Sign Vel never sees them. The app asks Google Play whether this Google account owns Pro each time it starts, which is why it has internet access. That check goes only to Google Play. Google’s handling of purchases is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What stays on your phone

The app saves your own settings on your device so they are there next time: the lock style and darkness, how you unlock, the clock style and format, brightness, sleep timer and vibration options, and the floating button’s look. It also keeps simple usage totals for the Stats tab (how many times and how long the screen was locked). None of this leaves your phone, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Display over other apps: shows the floating button and the black, touch-blocking screen on top of YouTube or any other app. The app does not read or record what is on your screen.
  • Run in the foreground and show notifications: keeps the floating button working while you use other apps, and shows a notification with Lock screen and Stop buttons.
  • Set wallpaper: used only when you tap Home, Lock or Both to set a black wallpaper.
  • Fingerprint, face or PIN (Pro, optional): secure unlock shows your phone’s own lock check. Android does the checking; the app only learns whether it passed and never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Internet and Google Play Billing: used for Pro purchases and for the usage statistics described above.

The app also uses the phone’s proximity sensor when you turn on pocket auto-lock (Pro), to notice when the phone goes into a pocket. When the sleep timer ends, or when you use the media buttons, the app asks Android to pause, play or skip whatever is playing; it does not see which app or video that is.

Sharing

Sign Vel does not sell or share your data. Usage statistics go to Google Firebase and Pro payments to Google Play, which process them for us as described above.

Children

Parents often use Black Screen so a child can watch a video without tapping away from it. The app is not directed at children, has no accounts or ads, and its usage statistics contain no personal details; parents can switch them off under Stats > Share usage data.
