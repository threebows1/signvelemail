Install

Getting your signature into your mail client

Step-by-step guides for every mail app Sign Vel supports: on your computer, in the browser and on your phone. Pick yours below.

Desktop appsWeb mailPhones and tablets

Before you start

  1. Finish your signature in the editor. Every signature you save is on your Signatures page.
  2. Most apps take Copy signature, which you paste like normal text. Thunderbird takes Export HTML instead. Each guide says which.
  3. Paste straight after copying, without copying anything else in between.
  4. When you are done, send a test email to yourself.

Desktop apps

Outlook, Apple Mail and Thunderbird on your computer.

Outlook — new Outlook for Windows and Outlook on the web

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, click the Settings gear at the top right.
  3. Go to Account → Signatures.
  4. Click + New signature and give it a name.
  5. Click in the signature box and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  6. Under Select default signatures, pick it for New messages and for Replies/forwards.
  7. Click Save. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Round corners turn square. Outlook draws email with Microsoft Word, which ignores rounded corners, so round icons and a round photo arrive square. Layout, colours, links and images come through.

Outlook — classic desktop app for Windows

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, go to File → Options → Mail, then click Signatures…
  3. On the E-mail Signature tab, click New and give it a name.
  4. Click in the Edit signature box and paste with Ctrl+V.
  5. On the right, choose the email account, then set New messages and Replies/forwards to this signature.
  6. Click OK, then OK again. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Using several computers? Classic Outlook keeps signatures on each computer, so repeat these steps on each one. Round corners turn square here too.

Outlook for Mac

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Outlook, go to Outlook → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Click + to add a signature and give it a name.
  4. Click in the signature box and paste with Cmd+V.
  5. Close the editing window to save it.
  6. Back in Signatures, under Choose default signature, pick it for new messages and for replies/forwards. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.

Apple Mail on a Mac

Full support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, go to Mail → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Pick your email account in the left column, then click + at the bottom of the middle column.
  4. Give the signature a name.
  5. Untick Always match my default message font. If it stays ticked, Mail changes your fonts.
  6. Delete the sample text in the right column and paste with Cmd+V.
  7. Under Choose Signature, pick your new signature. Close the window; it saves by itself. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.

Thunderbird

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel, click Export HTML, then Copy HTML. Thunderbird needs the HTML code, not a normal paste.
  2. In Thunderbird, open Account Settings (right-click your account in the left column).
  3. Click your email address at the top of the list.
  4. Under Signature text, tick Use HTML.
  5. Click in the box and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  6. Close the settings tab; it saves by itself. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.

Web mail

Mail you open in a browser.

Gmail on the web, including Google Workspace

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Gmail in a browser, click the Settings gear, then See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  4. Click + Create new, type a name and click Create.
  5. Click in the box beside it and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  6. Under Signature defaults, pick it for For new emails use and On reply/forward use.
  7. Scroll to the very bottom and click Save Changes. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Each person sets their own. In Google Workspace, every member of the team installs their signature this way. Google’s admin-wide footer only takes plain text, so it cannot hold a designed signature.

Yahoo Mail

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Yahoo Mail, click Settings (the gear), then More Settings.
  3. Choose Writing email.
  4. Under Signature, turn the switch on for your email address.
  5. Click in the box and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  6. It saves by itself. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.

iCloud Mail on the web

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. Go to icloud.com/mail and sign in.
  3. Click the Settings gear at the top of the mailbox list, then Settings.
  4. Choose Composing and turn on the signature.
  5. Click in the box and paste with Cmd+V (Ctrl+V on Windows).
  6. Click Done. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Check the spacing. iCloud on the web sometimes trims spacing on paste. If it looks tight, the Mac and iPhone Mail apps keep it better.

Proton Mail

Good support
  1. Open your signature in Sign Vel and click Copy signature.
  2. In Proton Mail, open Settings → All settings.
  3. Go to Identity and addresses.
  4. Click in the Email signature box and paste with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Click Update. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Images may be held back. Proton protects its users from remote images, so some recipients see your logo only after they choose to load images.

Phones and tablets

Phone apps keep their own signature, separate from the one on your computer.

Mail on iPhone and iPad

Good support
  1. On the phone, open your signatures in the browser, sign in, open the signature and tap Copy signature.
  2. Open the Settings app, go to Apps → Mail (on older iOS just Mail), then tap Signature.
  3. Choose All Accounts or Per Account, and delete the “Sent from my iPhone” text.
  4. Touch and hold in the box, then tap Paste.
  5. If it turns plain, shake the phone straight away and tap Undo. The iPhone then keeps the design.
  6. Go back to save. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.

Gmail app on iPhone and Android

Text only
  1. Open the Gmail app, tap the menu (three lines), then Settings.
  2. Tap your email address.
  3. Tap Mobile signature (on iPhone, Signature settings, then turn it on).
  4. Type a short signature — your name, role and phone number — and tap OK.
The Gmail app cannot show a designed signature. It only allows plain text on the phone. Your full signature, set up in Gmail on the web, is still used whenever you send from a computer.

Outlook app on iPhone and Android

Partial support
  1. On the phone, open your signatures in the browser, sign in, open the signature and tap Copy signature.
  2. Open the Outlook app, tap your profile picture at the top left, then the Settings gear.
  3. Tap Signature.
  4. Delete the “Get Outlook for…” text, then touch and hold and tap Paste.
  5. Go back to save. Send a test email to yourself, and to an address on another service, to check how it looks.
Expect a simpler version. The Outlook phone app keeps text and links but can drop images and layout. Your full signature, set up in Outlook on a computer, is still used whenever you send from there.
